\section{Scope and Progress Against the Proposal}
\label{sec:scope}

\subsection{Project Context, Target Users and Current Scope}
IELTS Evaluator Pro is an online learning platform intended to help learners
connect assessment feedback with appropriate practice. It supports three
roles: learners study and submit work, instructors manage content and review
assessments, and administrators manage accounts and system configuration.

The initial design covered all four IELTS skills. The current MVP prioritises
a Writing Task~2 clinic within the core LMS: submission, evidence-linked
feedback, instructor review, targeted practice, revision and a new-topic task.
Provisional AI feedback is distinguished from official teacher-confirmed
results; only accepted and released evidence updates the official skill
profile. A Reading demonstration remains available. This narrower IELTS
focus does not remove the mandatory LMS requirements; unfinished core items
must remain visible in the implementation status and completion plan.

\paragraph{Deviation Analysis.}
The main foundations and selected integrated flows are implemented, but full
acceptance, public deployment and independent evaluation remain outstanding.
The remaining plan prioritises mandatory LMS gaps, a reproducible three-role
demo, permitted expert-labelled data and comparisons against declared
baselines. Synthetic checks support pipeline verification and do not
establish scoring accuracy or learning gains. Section~\ref{sec:plan}
provides proposed responsibilities, risks and recovery milestones.
